LEGO Hands

Your camera tracks your hand in real time — the video is never shown, only your hand's position is used to move the bricks.

1Tap Start, then allow camera access. The feed stays invisible.
2Move your hand — a glowing ring follows your palm on screen.
3Pinch (thumb + index) over a brick to lift it, move, then open your pinch to drop.
4Pinch a brick in the bottom tray to pull out a new one. Or use mouse / touch.

How to play

  • Keep your hand in front of the camera (front camera, mirrored).
  • The ring follows your palm. Pinch thumb + index to grab.
  • Open the pinch to drop the brick — it falls and stacks.
  • Pull a brick from the tray at the bottom, or tap it to spawn one.
  • Desktop: move the mouse, hold the button to grab.
  • Needs HTTPS on a phone (camera access). GitHub Pages, a tunnel, or localhost works.
